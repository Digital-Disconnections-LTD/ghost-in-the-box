# Ghost in the Box artwork

`ghost-in-the-box-garden.png` was created for this project with OpenAI image generation.
The friendly ghost, messenger envelopes, and glowing garden give Ghost in the Box
its own identity alongside Batbox. It is project artwork, rather than a claim of
affiliation with any social platform.

## Art direction

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool. The final prompt used the
Batbox banner as a ghost/style reference and the earlier garden composition as
a scene reference:

> Beautiful wide GitHub repository hero banner, landscape 3:1. Rebrand the local
> community social publishing tool as “GHOST IN THE BOX”. Use the friendly smiling
> luminous ivory ghost: rounded body, tiny dark eyes, open happy smile, warm inner
> glow, raised little arms. A magical futuristic midnight conversation garden,
> glowing speech-bubble plants, message envelopes, warm lanterns, purple and midnight
> blue sky. Polished 3D storybook cinematic style. On the right, the ghost floats
> joyfully out of an open dark violet box with a luminous teal seam and amber inner
> light, tending the garden of conversation bubbles. Tiny envelopes travel on light
> trails. No owl, sprout humanoid, or bat: this ghost has its own project identity.
> Reserve the left 55% for legible futuristic ivory type: “GHOST” on the first line,
> “IN THE BOX” on the second, and “Grow a conversation.” below. Wide margins. A few
> pumpkins and warm Halloween lanterns. No platform logos, watermarks, or other text.
